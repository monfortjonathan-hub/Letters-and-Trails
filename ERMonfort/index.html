About this map

Elias Riggs Monfort (1842–1920) enlisted as a private in Co. A, 6th Ohio, on 18 June 1861, was commissioned into Co. F, 75th Ohio, in October 1861, and was severely wounded in the hip and thigh at Gettysburg on 2 July 1863 — the bullet driving fragments of his own sword into the wound. He was discharged 9 December 1863.
